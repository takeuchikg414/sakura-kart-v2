What is SAKURA KART?

Your Tokyo trip,
now with an opening theme. あなたの東京旅行に、
オープニング曲を。

SAKURA KART is a guided street go-kart tour for international visitors. You drive your own single-seat kart on real public roads, following a guide through Tokyo's most famous spots.

Pick a costume, mount an action cam, and roll out. People wave, take photos, cheer you on. For one hour, you're the main character.

SAKURA KART は、訪日旅行者のためのガイド付き公道カートツアーです。1人乗りのカートを自分で運転し、ガイドの後ろについて東京の名所を走ります。

衣装を選んで、アクションカメラを付けて、いざ出発。街の人が手を振り、写真を撮り、応援してくれる。その1時間、あなたは主人公です。

Required license

NO LICENSE,
NO DRIVE!

To drive on Japanese roads, bring one of these sets, in the original. Digital licenses and copies are not accepted.

TYPE 1

International Driving Permit

IDP (1949 Geneva Convention) + home license + passport

Paper booklet only. IDPs can't be issued in Japan, so get yours before you fly.

TYPE 2

Home license + translation

Home license + official Japanese translation + passport

For Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Monaco and Taiwan. Translations are available from JAF and others.

TYPE 3

US SOFA license

SOFA / military license or US state license + military ID

For US Forces Japan personnel and their families.

TYPE 4

Japanese license

Japanese regular license (AT OK)

Moped and motorcycle licenses are not valid.

Not sure? After booking, email a photo of your documents to and we'll check them for you. 不安な方は、予約後に書類の写真を に送ってください。事前に確認します。

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Can anyone drive?
Our karts are automatic, so if you hold a license valid in Japan you're good to go. Visitors usually need an International Driving Permit under the 1949 Geneva Convention. Moped and motorcycle licenses don't count. License guide
Is there an age limit?
You need a license valid in Japan, so in practice riders are 18+.
Is insurance included?
Yes. A standard plan is included, but there's a deductible (¥50,000 per kart) if the kart gets damaged. Add full coverage when booking to bring it down to zero.
Can I ride with a passenger?
All karts are single-seat, and every rider needs a valid license.
How big can our group be?
For safety, up to 6 drivers per guide. Bigger group? Contact us and we'll split you into waves a few minutes apart.
Can I change or cancel?
Yes, if there's space you can change the date, group size or route. Free up to 7 days before, after that the cancellation policy applies.
What if it rains?
Light rain? We ride (raincoats provided). In severe weather we'll contact you to reschedule or refund in full.
Is there parking?
No. Traffic can make you late, and late arrivals can't join, so please take the train.
Do we drive on highways?
No highways. The Rainbow Bridge route still feels like flying, though.
What should I wear?
Closed-toe shoes, please. Costumes are free to rent.
Are you connected to any video game or anime?
No. SAKURA KART is not affiliated with any video game, anime or character brand, and we don't provide copyrighted character costumes. Real roads have no reset button, so drive safe!
