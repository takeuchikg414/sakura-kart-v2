Terms of use

  1. Agreement

    Participants use the service on the condition that they agree to these terms. Without agreement, no service is provided.

  2. Who can ride

    Every participant must meet all of the following. If someone rides without meeting them, insurance will not apply.

    • Holds a license or permit valid in Japan (see the license guide)
    • Has enough driving skill to operate a kart safely
    • Is not under the influence of alcohol or drugs
    • Understands that SAKURA KART is not affiliated with any video game, anime or character brand

  3. Traffic laws

    Participants must obey Japanese traffic laws and carry their license at all times while driving.

  4. Pre-ride kart check

    Under Japanese law the driver is responsible for checking that the kart is roadworthy (lights, indicators, brakes, etc.). Tell staff immediately if anything seems wrong.

  5. Traffic violations

    Each driver is responsible for their own violations. SAKURA KART and its guides are not responsible for resulting fines or fees, and may charge the participant for any that are billed to us.

  6. If there's an accident

    Report any accident to your guide, the police and the insurer. Do not agree to a settlement with the other party without our consent. We are not responsible for settlements made without it.

  7. Insurance & deductible

    All karts are insured, within the limits of the policy. On the standard plan, the driver pays a deductible of ¥50,000 per kart if an accident occurs. Adding full coverage at booking removes the deductible. Reckless driving may void coverage.

  8. Kart damage

    Participants are responsible for damage to the kart, and we may charge the actual cost of repair. Damage covered by insurance follows the previous article.

  9. No criminal organizations

    Participants confirm they are not members of, and are not involved with, criminal organizations or criminal activity.

  10. No swapping or passengers

    You may not let anyone else drive or ride your kart unless the shop or guide says so.

  11. No commercial use

    Karts may not be used for commercial purposes (delivery, advertising, filming, etc.) without our permission.

  12. No modifications

    Do not modify the kart (stickers, devices, etc.) without consent. Action cameras may only be mounted in a way that doesn't interfere with safe driving.

  13. Photos & video

    Participants will not make copyright or portrait-right claims over photos and videos we take during the tour. We may use them for promotion. If you prefer not to appear, tell staff on the day.

  14. Guide's decision

    If we judge there is a safety risk (reckless driving, ignoring tour rules, etc.), the shop and guide may stop a participant from driving. No refund is given in that case.

  15. Late arrival

    If you miss the check-in time (30 minutes before start), you may not be able to join, and it will be treated as a no-show.

  16. Weather

    Tours run in light rain. If we decide conditions are unsafe (typhoon, heavy rain, snow, etc.), we cancel and offer a reschedule or full refund.

  17. Compensation

    If a participant breaches these terms and causes us loss, the participant will compensate for it.

  18. Disclaimer

    Except in cases of our intentional misconduct or gross negligence, we are not liable for damages suffered by participants. We may change or suspend services.

  19. Media

    Media may cover our tours on some days. Let staff know if you don't want to be filmed.

  20. Governing law

    These terms are governed by Japanese law, and the Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction in the first instance. If the Japanese and English versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.
