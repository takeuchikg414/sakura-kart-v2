Privacy policy

  1. What we collect

    Name, email, phone number, country, driver's license and passport details, booking details, and payment information (card numbers are handled by our payment provider; we do not store them).

  2. Why we use it

    To take and confirm bookings, contact you, verify eligibility to drive, handle insurance and accidents, improve our service, and send news (only if you opt in).

  3. Sharing

    We don't share your data without consent, except as required by law, for accident reports to insurers and police, and for payment processing.

  4. Storage & deletion

    We keep data only as long as needed and then delete it. To request access, correction or deletion, contact us below.
