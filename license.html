SAKURA KART runs on Japanese public roads, so every driver must hold a license or permit that is valid in Japan.

Type 1: International Driving PermitTYPE 1：国際運転免許証（IDP）

What to bring

  • International Driving Permit issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention
  • Your home-country driver's license (used to match the IDP)
  • Passport

A valid IDP for Japan must meet ALL of these

  1. Issued by a country that is party to the 1949 Geneva Convention on Road Traffic (e.g. Mexico, Kuwait and Saudi Arabia are not).

  2. Issued by the officially recognized body (e.g. AAA in the US, CAA in Canada, the Post Office in the UK). Beware of fake “IDPs” sold online.

  3. A paper booklet. Card-type, digital, single-sheet and photocopied permits are not valid.

  4. Shows the 1949 Convention (often printed on the cover). If yours says “1968”, contact us first.

  5. Category B (cars) is stamped or marked.

  6. Your ride date is within 1 year of the IDP issue date, and within 1 year of your entry into Japan.

  7. IDPs cannot be issued in Japan. Get yours at home before you travel.

Type 2: Home license + official translationTYPE 2：本国の免許証＋公式日本語翻訳文

For license holders from Switzerland, Germany, France, Belgium, Monaco and Taiwan.

What to bring

  • Your home-country driver's license (digital licenses not accepted)
  • Official Japanese translation of that license
  • Passport

Where to get the translation

The Japan Automobile Federation (JAF), your country's embassy or consulate in Japan, ADAC (Germany), the Taiwan-Japan Relations Association and others. Valid for 1 year from your entry into Japan.

Type 3: US Forces Japan (SOFA)TYPE 3：在日米軍関係者（SOFA）

What to bring

  • SOFA license (USFJ Form 4EJ) / military license, or a US state driver's license
  • DoD / military ID, or documents proving you belong to one of the groups below

Who qualifies

  • Active-duty members of the US armed forces stationed in Japan
  • US-national civilian employees serving with or accompanying US forces in Japan (excluding ordinary residents of Japan)
  • Spouses and dependents of the above

Type 4: Japanese driver's licenseTYPE 4：日本の運転免許証

A Japanese regular (ordinary) license is fine, including automatic-only. Moped and motorcycle licenses are not valid.

Japanese licenses are issued to permanent and mid-to-long-term residents. To convert a foreign license, contact your local driver's license center.
